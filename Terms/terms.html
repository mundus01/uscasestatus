What this service is

uscasestatus retrieves the publicly available status of USCIS cases by receipt number and presents it with context: what the status means, how many comparable cases are ahead of yours, how quickly comparable cases are being decided, and an estimated range for when something may happen next. You may optionally create an account to track cases, receive alerts, and add details about your filing.

Accuracy and estimates

  • Case statuses come from USCIS systems. If USCIS is wrong, delayed, or unavailable, so are we.
  • USCIS publishes only a case's current status, not its full history. Events before we began observing a case may be missing.
  • Queue positions and pace figures are derived from cases we observe in a receipt block. USCIS does not decide cases in strict receipt order, so a queue position is a reference point, not a countdown.
  • Estimates are statistical ranges describing groups of cases. They are not predictions about your case and are not guarantees. Your case may be decided much sooner or much later than any range we show.
  • Approval rates describe historical outcomes for comparable cases. They say nothing about the merits of your case.
  • We may be wrong. If you find an error, please tell us.

Do not make legal, financial, travel, employment, or immigration decisions based solely on information from this site.
